    Steps

    Each step is compared with the state before it, and held against its claim where it has one. Step 0 checks that the published results are reproduced; it is the start, not the goal.

    The whole way

    Every difference between the first state and the last is the sum of the steps' differences. "Largest step" counts, per indicator, the items whose largest share comes from each step, of the steps that moved them; "spread over steps" counts the items whose whole way moved while no single step did. The split is exact up to rounding, and it depends on the order of the steps where two steps touch the same results.

    Matrix

    Each cell is the result after divided by the result before, for one item and indicator, in the comparison chosen above. A heading sorts by how far the two lie apart, the furthest first. Choose a row (click it, or press Enter on it) to open the item.

    Distributions

    Values per class and band, as a share of all values of the indicator. Every indicator is drawn on the same scale.
