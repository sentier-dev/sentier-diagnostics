    Steps

    Each step is compared with the state before it, and held against its claim where it has one. Step 0 checks that the published results are reproduced; it is the start, not the goal.

    The whole way

    Every difference between the first state and the last is the sum of the steps' differences. "Largest step" counts, per indicator, the items whose largest share comes from each step. The split is exact, and it depends on the order of the steps where two steps touch the same results.

    Distributions

    Values per class and band, as a share of all values of the indicator. Every indicator is drawn on the same scale.
